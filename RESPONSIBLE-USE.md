# Responsible Use

This repository contains software components and product material related to online card games. Technology alone does not determine whether a deployment is lawful, fair, secure, or permitted by a platform.

Do not use the project to facilitate unlawful gambling, evade platform controls, manipulate users, conceal material rules, misuse personal data, or infringe third-party code and assets.

Before deployment, obtain appropriate legal and security review for:

- local gambling, sweepstakes, contest, prize, age, and geolocation rules;
- payments, virtual currency, withdrawal, anti-fraud, AML/KYC, taxation, and consumer protection;
- privacy, analytics, advertising, account deletion, and cross-border data transfer;
- random-number generation, game fairness, collusion, bots, auditability, and dispute handling;
- Apple, Google, payment-provider, advertising-network, and hosting policies;
- licenses for code, art, audio, fonts, brands, SDKs, and other third-party material.

The maintainers do not represent that the public repository is complete, audited, production-ready, or approved for any jurisdiction. Users are responsible for their own technical, legal, security, and compliance decisions.
